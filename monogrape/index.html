How it works

We compressed the entire global produce supply chain into three taps. Then we removed the part where you get a useful amount of produce.

1

Request

Open the app and tap the grape. There is only one button. Our design team spent eight months removing the others.

2

Fulfillment

A picker selects your grape by hand at one of our regional micro-vineyards. It is weighed, chilled, photographed for your records, and placed in its cradle.

3

Delivery

A courier carries your grape in a temperature-controlled single-grape backpack and presents it to you. Average handoff time: 11 seconds. Some customers applaud.

The unboxing is the point

Every Monogrape arrives in our signature rigid box with four layers of lavender tissue paper, a molded paper cradle, and a card noting your grape's vineyard row and picking time. The grape-to-packaging ratio is 1 to 340 by weight. We consider this our moat.

  • 14-point firmness and gloss inspection
  • Cold chain maintained at 4°C from vine to hand
  • Provenance card with picker's initials
  • Tissue paper is compostable, eventually, somewhere

A courier network built for one

Our couriers complete a three-week certification covering grape handling, cadence of approach, and the handoff itself, which has choreography. Each carries the G1, our custom insulated backpack with room for exactly one box. Engineers proposed a two-box version in 2024. They no longer work here.

  • Live GPS tracking down to the grape
  • Photo confirmation of the presented grape
  • In-app tipping, with a suggested 45%
