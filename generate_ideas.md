# Generate Ideas

Instructions for generating fake business ideas.

Each business should be a joke or humorous in some way.

# Common Themes

Use these ideas categories for inspiration.  You can use one by itself or mix and match (like a company with gross flavors that also has a ridiculous subscription).  

Feel free to choose ideas that dont map directly to any theme.

1. Gross flavors of food or drinks, such as bug flavored ice cream, or thanksgiving entree sports drinks.
2. Simply gross.  For example, a booger donation service ("we need your mucus").
3. Extra dark humor, like a company selling sadistic mouse traps that are designed like medieval torture devices, or a company that sells bedazzled body bags.
4. Ultra luxury where no market exists (or should exist), like a company selling ultra premium cat litter made from crushed crystal, marble, etc.
5. Companies that sell a useless product, like calculators without all 9 numbers.
6. Companies of dubious legality.  For example, a company that will modify road signs for you.
7. Ridiculous subscription businesses.  For example, a company selling fake house plants w/ a watering or maintenance package.
8. Incredibly mundane ideas played off as a hot tech company or startup.  For example, a company that sells guides on how to make toast.
9. Large ideas or products played off as a "mom and pop" or small town business.  For example, a boutique microchip design company, or an enterprise software company that looks like a mom and pop shop.

# Other Notes

1. Companies can be non-profits or for profits.  They can be large (faceless mega corps) or tiny one man operations.
2. They can be anywhere in the US or world, especially if there could be a joke there.

# Example Businesses

These are examples - do not reproduce as actual ideas.

1. Critter Creamery. An ice cream parlor specializing in bug flavored ice cream.
2. Coal Emporium. A company that delivers coal as gifts to naughty children and adults, with multiple premium options.
3. Morning Momentum.  A company that sells pictures of my morning oatmeal, treated like a hot tech company.
4. Litter Reserve. A company that sells luxury cat litter made of different luxury materials.

# Rules

1. No direct references to existing business or corporations.
2. Dont use the same name as an existing business.
3. Do not repeat any ideas.  Read through the list of all ideas in "business_ideas.md" to make sure you are not repeating any.

# Generating the Name

For each business, come up with the following fields:

1. A unique name.
2. Short description.  The one sentence description.
3. Long description. A few sentences describing what the business does in more detail.
4. Themes and joke.  A short description of the major themes and the main joke.

# Finalizing

Write each completed idea to "business_ideas.md".  Append, do not delete any ideas.  Merge to main when done.
