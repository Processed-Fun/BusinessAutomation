The Perfectly Balanced guarantee

Never uniform

Every face is a different size. We measure them after we cut them, out of curiosity, not obligation.

Never balanced

Each die ships with a Fairness Report. Every report says the same thing. It says No.

Never sanded

Sanding is how a die learns to lie about what it is. Our founder has not sanded anything since 1987, including this website.

Always heavy

Dice over 20 pounds should be rolled from a seated position. Dice over 100 pounds are also furniture.
