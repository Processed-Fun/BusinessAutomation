Questions

Frequently Asked

Who actually pays for the birdhouse?

The buyer. Typically this is the human who owns the garden, purchasing on behalf of a bird they hope to attract, which is the oldest arrangement in real estate: buying something beautiful so that someone you admire might live near you.

Do the birds know?

The birds know everything. They simply decline to be involved in the paperwork.

What is seed pricing, exactly?

All listings are denominated in seed, the only currency the end user recognizes. Our closing office accepts conventional payment and performs the conversion at the prevailing dawn rate. The rate is posted nowhere and disputed by no one.

Why are all showings at dawn?

Light, honesty, and song. A birdhouse at dawn shows you what it is. A birdhouse at noon shows you what it wants you to think it is. We also find that buyers who arrive at 5:40 a.m. are serious.

Is financing available?

Yes. Our partners at First Fledgling Savings & Loan offer a standard 30-season fixed with excellent terms for first-time nesters. Note that a season is not a year, a distinction the bank discovered late and now describes as a feature.

What does an inspection cover?

Post or bough integrity, drainage, entry-hole diameter certification, and a full woodpecker report. If the inspector taps the structure and something taps back, that is disclosed as occupancy, not as a defect.

What is your position on squirrels?

The firm does not represent squirrels. The firm has been asked to represent squirrels many times, always by the same squirrel, and the answer remains no. This is covered at length in our fair housing statement.

Can I live in one of your listings?

Our residences range from 48 to 96 square inches. We admire the ambition and must decline the showing.

What happens if a house sells but the bird never moves in?

This is the quiet heartbreak of our industry. The listing remains sold, the garden remains hopeful, and Meredith sends a card each spring until someone with feathers changes their mind. No placement has gone unfilled longer than two seasons. We do not intend to start now.
