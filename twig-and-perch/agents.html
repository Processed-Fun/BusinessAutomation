Meet the Agents

Three agents. No teams, no assistants, no lockboxes. Every showing is attended in person, at dawn, in sensible shoes.

Meredith Larkspur

Principal Broker

Sixteen years placing discerning cavity-nesters in the region’s finest addresses. Meredith closed the record-setting sale of the Dovecote at Quail Ridge and has personally walked every canopy she represents. She is committed to finding the right fit for you and your flock.

Theodore Finch

Senior Associate, Alpine & Woodland

A former field ornithologist, Theodore left academia when he realized birds needed representation more than they needed observation. He specializes in high-altitude and treeline properties, and remains committed to finding the right fit for you and your flock.

1-800-TWIG, extension 2

Constance Dove

Associate, Heritage & Gourd

Constance leads our heritage practice, covering cured gourds, estate hollows, and homes of unusual provenance. Her staging work with fresh moss is quietly famous. She is, above all, committed to finding the right fit for you and your flock.
