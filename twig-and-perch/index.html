For Buyers

How a placement works.

Tour at dawn

All showings are held at first light, when the property sings. Arrive early. The light does not wait, and neither do the birds.

Offer in seed

We present your offer the same morning. Financing is available through our partners at First Fledgling Savings & Loan, on a standard 30-season fixed.

Close by molt

Inspection, woodpecker report, and title search on the post or bough. Most placements close within a single molt, keys, such as they are, at closing.
