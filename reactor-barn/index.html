The folks

Three of us, plus the dog

You'll deal with a person here, and probably a person you'll deal with again for thirty years. The dog is named Becquerel. He is not allowed in the dry storage area and he knows it.

Hank Sr.

Founded the shop in 1987 after the feed business got slow and he "had all this room." Handles thermal hydraulics, core design, and complaints about the website.

Hank Jr.

Runs the front counter, the punch cards, and now this website, which took him most of a winter. If you call, you get Hank Sr. If you email, you get Hank Jr. in three to five days.

Cousin Denise

Welds containment domes on weekends. Her seams have outlasted two tornadoes and every one of her marriages. Ask about the barn quilt paint option, she's proud of it.

Pricing, plainly

No tiers, no bundles, one punch card

Every unit on the lot has its price painted right on it. Backyard units start at $4,000 for the Fixer-Upper and top out around $61,500 for the Grain Silo. The Church Social runs $240,000 installed. The County Line is a sit-down conversation.

Anything you can load on a wagon, you can buy right here on the website. Anything that needs a crane, a permit hearing, or a casserole schedule starts with a phone call.

And yes, the punch card is real. Buy nine reactors and the tenth is half off. One co-op has done it. They got a plaque.
