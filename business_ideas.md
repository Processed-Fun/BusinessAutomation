## Blinkr

**Short description:** A venture-backed startup whose app reminds you to blink.

**Long description:** Blinkr is a Series B "ocular wellness platform" that sends push notifications telling you to blink, something you were already doing. The founders describe blinking as "a legacy manual process ripe for disruption" and tout their proprietary BlinkScore, streak tracking, and a premium tier that reminds you slightly more often. The pitch deck claims a total addressable market of "everyone with eyes," and investors have valued the company at $1.4 billion. The app's only setting is off, which is Pro-only.

**Themes and joke:** Incredibly mundane idea played as a hot tech startup (theme 8). The joke is the full startup ceremony — metrics, paywalls, disruption language — around an involuntary reflex.

**Archetype:** saas-product

**Name check note (Sep 2026):** A real app named Blinkr (getblinkr.com) exists with the same name and the same premise, an app that reminds you to blink. Rename or rework this idea before building it.


## Fines & Dandy

**Short description:** A premium cereal brand that sells only the crumb dust from the bottom of the bag.

**Long description:** Fines & Dandy harvests what the industry calls "fines" — the crumb dust that settles at the bottom of every cereal bag — and sells it as the main event. The lineup is organized in single-origin varieties by parent cereal, each with tasting notes ("forward notes of toasted corn, a lingering finish of bag"), pour-shot photography, and pairing recipes for milk, yogurt, and "dry, by the handful." A store locator shows the brand stocked exclusively on the bottom shelf.

**Themes and joke:** The worst part of a product, packaged as premium (themes 4 and 5). The joke is complete craft-food-brand ceremony — provenance, tasting notes, beauty shots — around sweepings.

**Archetype:** cpg-brand


## The Parting Shot

**Short description:** A high-end photography studio specializing in divorce shoots.

**Long description:** The Parting Shot brings the full wedding-photography treatment to the other end of the arc: editorial coverage of the signing day, "first look at the paperwork" sessions, tasteful courthouse-steps portraiture, and commemorative albums with foil-stamped covers. Package tiers range from a single "Decree in Golden Hour" session to full-day coverage including both attorneys. The portfolio is presented with the hushed, romantic tone of a fine-art wedding studio, applied without comment to its subject.

**Themes and joke:** Dark humor played straight (theme 3). The joke is wedding photography's entire visual and verbal genre — the reverence, the storytelling, the albums — aimed at the opposite occasion.

**Archetype:** studio-portfolio
