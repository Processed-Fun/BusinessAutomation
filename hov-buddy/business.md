# HOV Buddy

**Short description:** A rental service supplying realistic mannequin passengers for carpool-lane commuters.

**Long description:** HOV Buddy rents hyper-realistic passengers by the week so solo drivers can "carpool with confidence." The lineup includes Nodding Greg, Sleeping Aunt Carol, and the deluxe Conversational Dave, whose head turns at stoplights. Plans include seasonal outfit swaps, a "traffic stop protection plan" that the terms of service admit protects nothing, and an emergency hotline that only plays hold music. The website's legal page is a single shrug emoticon.

**Themes and joke:** Dubious legality (theme 6) with a ridiculous subscription layer. The joke is a company building tiered pricing, accessories, and customer support around what is straightforwardly a traffic violation.

**Archetype:** landing-page

**Style:** startup-modern
