The Deluxe Experience

Dave's Head Turns at Stoplights

A small servo, a light sensor, and eleven degrees of rotation. When the light goes red, Dave turns toward you like he has been waiting all day to finish the boat story. When it goes green, he returns to the road, satisfied.

Renters tell us the motion sells the whole illusion. One reported that after three weeks she started answering him. We told her that was normal. We are not sure it is normal.
