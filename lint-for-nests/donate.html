Donate to Lint for Nests

Lint keeps the birds warm, but money keeps the lint moving. Your gift funds sorting tables, sanitation cycles, jar deposits, and the modest stipend of our one part-time driver, Gary.

Make a gift

Monthly donors at $15 and up receive the official window decal. Donors at $40 and up will have a wild warbler named after them within two migration cycles.

Deborah II. Named for a donor from Cedar Rapids. She does not know this, and that is fine.

Where your money goes

  • 62% sorting, sanitation, and nest installation
  • 21% Lint Drive logistics and folding tables
  • 9% donor services and certificate calligraphy
  • 8% Gary
For distinguished navels

The Golden Navel Circle

Our most devoted supporters give $2,500 a year and receive what money can rarely buy: recognition among peers who understand. Members are invited to the annual Sorting Gala in Oskaloosa, receive priority naming rights during warbler season, and are listed, or pointedly not listed, in the annual report according to their wishes.

Membership is by inquiry. A senior gift officer will contact you discreetly, as is our way.
