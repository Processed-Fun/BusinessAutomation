Store locator

Fines & Dandy is stocked in fine grocers nationwide, exclusively on the bottom shelf. This is a brand decision, not a negotiating failure. Enter your ZIP code to find your nearest settle.

    All locations stock the full lineup unless the Cinnamon Square has already gone, which it has.

    Why the bottom shelf?

    Fines settle. It's what they do, and we honor it. Placing our jars at eye level would betray everything the product stands for, so every retail partner signs an agreement keeping Fines & Dandy within fourteen inches of the floor. Kneel. It's worth it.

    "If you're not crouching, you're not shopping."
