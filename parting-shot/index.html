The Heirloom

The Commemorative Album

Every package concludes with an album: museum-grade paper, hand-bound in black leather, your names and dates foil-stamped in gold. One copy each, printed simultaneously, so neither of you can claim the original.

Titles are set in-house. Recent volumes include "The Uncoupling of Margaret & Paul: A Study in Golden Hour."
