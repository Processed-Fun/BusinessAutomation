Our Craft

From Twelve States. From Thousands of Ears. For You.

Our wax benefactors visit donation lounges across twelve states, where their contributions are collected in comfort and, we cannot stress this enough, in private. The wax is then pooled, triple-filtered, and hand-poured into vessels worthy of its journey.

Most candle houses buy paraffin by the ton. We built relationships. There is a difference, and you can smell it.

Meet the Source

Gary Is Not Like Us

Every house has its legend. Ours is a retired insurance adjuster from Ohio whose yield our assessors describe, in writing, as "frankly unsettling." Gary asked us not to make a fuss. We made a line of candles instead.

Single-Origin Gary is released in numbered batches, four times a year, and sells out in hours. Gary remains humble. His ears remain undefeated.
