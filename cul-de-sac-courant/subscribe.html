The operation

Every subscription funds the same things it funded in 2011: paper, ink, one police scanner, and the standing archive of everything that has ever happened on Butternut Court, which fills a hall closet and is backed up to two locations, one of which is the other side of the closet. The Courant has never missed a Tuesday. It has come close twice, both times involving the printer, neither time involving the news, of which there is always more than eight houses should produce.
