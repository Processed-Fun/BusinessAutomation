# The Cul-de-Sac Courant

**Short description:** A hard-hitting subscription newspaper covering the eight houses of one suburban cul-de-sac.

**Long description:** The Courant is the paper of record for Butternut Court, delivering fearless investigative journalism about its eight households since 2011. Recent front pages include a five-part series on who keeps moving the Hendersons' gnome, live coverage of the Kowalskis' new mailbox, and an explosive exposé on the true start time of the Fourth of July potluck. The site has a metered paywall (three articles a month, and everyone on the street has hit it), breaking-news push alerts, an opinion section written entirely by one retiree, and a corrections page longer than most issues.

**Themes and joke:** Mundane suburban nothing covered with the full apparatus of serious journalism (theme 8, in newspaper form) plus a ridiculous subscription (theme 7). The joke is prestige-journalism gravity — paywalls, exposés, editorial independence — aimed at a street with eight houses.

**Archetype:** editorial

**Style:** broadsheet
