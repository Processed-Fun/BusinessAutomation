Promotion Department

House advertisements

The Courant accepts no outside advertising, so the back page runs these. The Promotion Department is the editor, on Sundays, with strong opinions about small caps. Residents may request a poster for their window; the Hendersons have not, and the Courant respects that, publicly.

Posters are printed on the same machine as the newspaper and are available at the newsroom door for $3, honor system, exact change appreciated because the change jar is also the petty cash system.
